READING THE MAPS

What these prototypes establish

Eleven views use eleven distinct idioms: line, stream, heatmap, bump, lollipop, box plot, graduated symbols, range-and-dot, slope, point map and choropleth. Each answers a different question.

OpenStreetMap is community maintained. Store locations and brand tags may be incomplete or outdated, so the access map should be described as an indicative view rather than an official store census.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Why this story, and who it is for

Why + who

Grocery prices affect every household, but headline inflation can hide differences between foods, retailers and neighbourhoods. This story is designed for Australian shoppers without specialist statistical knowledge.

What + how

ABS price, population and income data are combined with CHOICE basket prices and OpenStreetMap locations. Each idiom matches a task: compare, rank, trace change, locate stores or normalise access.
