READING THE MAPS

What these prototypes establish

Eleven views use eleven distinct idioms: line, 100% stacked area, heatmap, bump, lollipop, horizontal bar, graduated symbols, range-and-dot, slope, point map and choropleth. Each answers a different question.

OpenStreetMap is community maintained. Store locations and brand tags may be incomplete or outdated, so the access map should be described as an indicative view rather than an official store census.

Sources: ABS Consumer Price Index, ABS regional population and income, CHOICE supermarket basket survey, and OpenStreetMap contributors.
